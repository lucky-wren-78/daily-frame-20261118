2 MIDDLE EAST · WAR

Kuwait downs hostile drones as the US–Iran exchange widens

Kuwait said its defences intercepted hostile drones on Wednesday, naming no origin and reporting no casualties. Hours earlier US jets struck an IRGC missile site on Iran’s Qeshm Island after Iranian drones targeted a US radar station in Bahrain.

3 OCEANIA · SANCTIONS

Australia sanctions Israelis over West Bank settlements

Canberra blacklisted three individuals and four organisations it links to settlement expansion and violence against Palestinians, Foreign Minister Penny Wong announced — a day after Britain moved against eight settlement firms.

4 SPACE

Voyager 1 becomes the first object one light-day from Earth

At 10:16 UTC, NASA’s 1977 probe crossed a full light-day — some 16.1 billion miles out. Its radio signals, sent at the speed of light, now take a whole day to reach home.

Severe Cyclone Akvan churns toward India’s east coast

The Bay of Bengal storm is forecast to cross the Andhra coast on Friday between Nellore and Tuni at 120–130 km/h, gusting 145. Chennai–Yangon flights were cancelled as the coast braced.
