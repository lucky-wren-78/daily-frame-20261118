2 MIDDLE EAST · WAR

Iran widens the Gulf war to Kuwait and Bahrain

Iranian drones hit a US air base at Ali Al Salem — Tehran’s first strike on Kuwait since September — as Bahrain fended off a third Iranian attack in a week. Kuwait said its defences downed the drones; no casualties reported.

3 NORTH AMERICA · SENATE

Peltola flips Alaska, and the Senate goes 56–44

Democrat Mary Peltola beat Republican Dan Sullivan 50.1% to 45.8%, the AP projected — the last Senate race of the midterms decided. She is the first Alaska Native in the Senate; Sullivan has yet to concede.

4 NORTH AMERICA · WEATHER

“Rain bomb” floods South Florida

Overnight rain dumped up to 15.3 inches on Broward County, stranding hundreds of drivers and shutting every public school. Hollywood and the county declared emergencies; a synagogue roof collapsed, with no injuries.

5 NORTH AMERICA · CONGRESS

Jeffries nominated as first Black Speaker candidate

House Democrats chose Hakeem Jeffries as Speaker nominee 251–11, putting him on course to become the first Black Speaker in US history when the new Congress votes on 3 January. Katherine Clark becomes the first woman majority leader.

6 EUROPE · UKRAINE

EU proposes a €45bn loan for Ukraine in 2027

The Commission proposed a €45 billion loan covering two-thirds of Kyiv’s 2027 funding gap — €20bn for the budget, €25bn for arms — repayable only once Russia pays war reparations.

7 SOUTH AMERICA · ECONOMY

Brazil’s Senate passes Lula’s R$60bn fiscal package

The Senate approved the adjustment package 69–2, capping real spending growth at 2% a year. The bill goes to the lower house as the real steadies after its post-election slide.

8 ASIA · UNREST

Bangladesh deploys paramilitary as Hasina’s return looms

Hundreds of paramilitary troops deployed after ten days of clashes, arson and bomb blasts by supporters of exiled ex-PM Sheikh Hasina, who plans to return in December. A bus helper was burned to death in Pirojpur.

9 AFRICA · TENSIONS

Ethiopia and Eritrea mass troops on their border

Both armies have moved within striking distance along their shared border, monitors reported, raising fears of open war. Artillery fire was reported near Badme; Eritrea calls the reports propaganda.

10 ASIA · ECONOMY

Malaysia’s economy grows 5.5% on the AI hardware boom

Third-quarter GDP grew 5.5% year on year, beating the 5.3% estimate, as exports rose 25.2% — electrical and electronics shipments up 66.9% on demand for AI hardware.

11 AFRICA · DEFENCE

Morocco to build 40% of its defence needs by 2035

King Mohammed VI launched the “Morocco Offer” defence-industry roadmap, backed by 4 billion dirhams. It targets 50,000 jobs and over 65% of sector revenue from exports, starting with drones, cyber and AI.

12 NORTH AMERICA · MARKETS

Anthropic prices its IPO at $83 in an $81.3bn listing

The AI developer priced its Nasdaq debut at $83 a share — above the $70–78 range — in an enlarged 980-million-share offering. Demand ran at about six times the shares on offer; trading starts Thursday as ANT.

13 NORTH AMERICA · GAMES

GTA VI reviews land: a 99 on Metacritic

Embargoes lifted a day before launch, and Grand Theft Auto VI scored 99 — matching Ocarina of Time as the highest-rated game ever. IGN, GameSpot and Forbes all gave it 10/10; the main gripe is a 30fps target.
