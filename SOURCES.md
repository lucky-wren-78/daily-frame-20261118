# Sources — what each panel of entry.png is based on

All 13 stories happened, or were first reported, on Wednesday 18 November 2026
(in some time zone). Compiled 18 November 2026 from that day's reporting,
starting a few hours into the UTC day; every story is backed by at least two
independent sources. Numbering matches the numbered dots on the map and the
numbered chips on the cards. Stories still to happen later on the 18th at
compile time (a scheduled Mass, address, handover, semi-finals) are worded in
the image as the day's scheduled events, exactly as the sources frame them.

## 1 — APEC leaders open their summit in Shenzhen (lead)
The 33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting runs 18–19 November in Shenzhen,
chaired by Xi Jinping, with Donald Trump, Vladimir Putin and South Korean
President Lee Jae-myung among the leaders arriving on the 18th; Xi keynoted
the APEC CEO Summit's opening on the morning of the 18th, and a first-ever
Trump–Xi–Putin three-way meeting is scheduled for Thursday on the sidelines.
Sources: CDS World (updated 18 Nov), BigGo Finance, Global South World,
Aju Press; Le Monde (18 Nov) and Reuters on the week's schedule.

## 2 — Kuwait downs hostile drones as the US–Iran exchange widens
Kuwait said early on Wednesday (Kuwait time) that its defences intercepted
hostile drones, naming no origin and reporting no casualties, after US jets
struck an IRGC missile site on Qeshm Island in response to Iranian drones
targeting a US radar station in Bahrain.
Sources: Al Jazeera reporting carried by World Pulse Wire (18 Nov 2026);
News Central TV (18 Nov).

## 3 — Australia sanctions Israelis over West Bank settlements
Foreign Minister Penny Wong announced sanctions on three Israeli individuals
and four organisations over settlement expansion and violence against
Palestinians, a day after Britain sanctioned eight settlement firms.
Sources: Reuters (syndicated, Sydney dateline, Wednesday 18 Nov); Amnesty
International Australia (18 November 2026); BusinessWorld (18 Nov).

## 4 — Voyager 1 becomes the first object one light-day from Earth
NASA's Voyager 1 crossed one light-day from Earth on 18 November at
10:16:07 UTC — about 16.1 billion miles out — the first human-made object to
reach that distance; NASA marked it with its "Shine Bright" celebration.
Sources: NASA (Curious Universe), Astronomy.com, IFLScience, USA Today /
Detroit Free Press.

## 5 — Severe Cyclone Akvan churns toward India's east coast
Severe Cyclonic Storm Akvan over the Bay of Bengal is forecast to make
landfall around noon on 20 November between Nellore and Tuni at 120–130 km/h
gusting 145; Chennai–Yangon flights were cancelled.
Sources: India Meteorological Department, National Bulletin No. 14;
Global1.news (18 Nov 2026, citing The Hindu).

## 6 — Bezos says he was "heavily involved" in picking Pattinson as Bond
Jeff Bezos described being "heavily involved" in choosing Robert Pattinson
to succeed Daniel Craig as James Bond, as Amazon MGM runs the franchise.
Sources: Daily Maverick (18 Nov, via AfricaFrontPage); The Guardian (via
MediaAlertPress, 18 Nov).

## 7 — Iran's Quds Force chief in Baghdad amid a militia standoff
IRGC Quds Force commander Esmail Qaani visited Iraq for talks with Prime
Minister Ali al-Zaidi and Coordination Framework figures amid the standoff
over Kataib Hezbollah and the Jurf al-Nasr oil-smuggling network.
Sources: Al Jazeera (via 234radio, published 18 Nov 00:33 UTC); GazeWire
(18 Nov); MediaAlertPress (18 Nov).

## 8 — Floods swamp Indonesia as 14 provinces sit on rain alert
BMKG warned of heavy rain across 14 provinces for 18–19 November; BNPB
reported floods in Aceh, West Kalimantan and Bengkulu affecting some 11,000
families, and strong winds damaged 588 houses in West Bandung.
Sources: IndoNET's 18 Nov 2026 BMKG/BNPB briefing; Kaldera News (BMKG
Jabodetabek warning); Kita Indonesia Satu (BMKG warning).

## 9 — Hegseth in Bogotá to hand Colombia its first Hercules
US Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth was due in Bogotá on Wednesday to hand
over Colombia's first C-130 Hercules — two more are expected — and hold
security-cooperation talks, as announced Tuesday by Defence Minister Jorge
Eduardo Mora.
Sources: Reuters (published 18 Nov 02:01 UTC, widely syndicated); El
Comercio (18 Nov); El Espectador (18 Nov).

## 10 — St Peter's Basilica marks 400 years since its dedication
St Peter's Basilica was dedicated on 18 November 1626; Pope Leo XIV presides
at the 400th-anniversary Mass at 17:00 on 18 November 2026, the day after
returning to Rome from his South America tour.
Sources: National Catholic Register; Catholic Review / OSV News;
ad-hoc-news.de; Shalom World.

## 11 — Ruto addresses Kenya as anger over living costs boils
President William Ruto delivers his State of the Nation Address to a joint
sitting of Parliament in Nairobi on Wednesday 18 November at 14:30, brought
forward from Thursday, with about 265 days to the 2027 election.
Sources: People Daily (Parliament's confirmation, 17 Nov); Dawan Africa
preview; Capital FM via Kenyan Digest; The Standard.

## 12 — Brazil's para paddlers play for world final places in Pattaya
Cátia Oliveira (class 2) and Paulo Henrique Fonseca (class 7) play their
Para Table Tennis World Championships semi-finals in Pattaya on Wednesday
18 November, bronze already assured for both; the championships end Thursday.
Sources: Brazilian Paralympic Committee (CPB); Surto Olímpico; QNews.

## 13 — Trump meets Marcos in Manila, then flies on to APEC
President Trump holds bilateral talks and a working lunch with President
Ferdinand Marcos Jr. at Malacañang on Wednesday before flying to Shenzhen
for APEC — his second trip to China this year.
Sources: Reuters ("Trump to meet Marcos in Manila before China talks");
Asia Today (summit-week schedule); Le Monde (18 Nov).
